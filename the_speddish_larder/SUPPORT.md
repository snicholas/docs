## 📚 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. How does the "AI Chef" work?
The AI Chef analyzes the ingredients currently in your pantry and your specified preferences (e.g., vegan, quick meal) to generate unique, customized recipes. It tries to use what you have to minimize waste.

### 2. How do I share my pantry with my family?
Go to the **Family Dashboard** in the app menu. Tap "Create Family" to start a new group. Once created, you can invite members by entering their email address. They must have the app installed and be signed in to accept the invitation.

### 3. What is "Smart Organize"?
"Smart Organize" is an AI-powered feature in your Pantry screen. It scans your item list to find duplicates (e.g., "Rice 500g" and "Rice 1kg") and proposes merging them into a single entry with the correct total quantity.

### 4. Can I print my recipes?
Yes! In the Recipe or Meal Planner screen, tap the **PDF** icon. You can then save, share, or print the generated PDF file.

### 5. Why do I see ads?
We use ads to support the development and maintenance of the app, including the costs of AI generation. 
- **Banner Ads:** Appear in some lists.
- **Rewarded Ads:** You may be asked to watch a short video to unlock premium features like PDF export or extra AI recipe generations if you are on the free plan.

### 6. How do I delete my account?
We value your privacy. You can delete your account and all associated data permanently from within the app:
1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Tap on **User Account**.
3. Select **Delete Account**.
*Note: This action is irreversible.*
