## 2. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

*   **Q: My game progress was lost! What happened?**
    *   A: The Speddish Orbis uses cloud saves via Firebase. Ensure you are logged into the same account you used previously. If you are playing as a guest, progress is tied to your device and may be lost if the app is uninstalled or device is reset.
*   **Q: I'm not seeing any ads.**
    *   A: Ad display depends on various factors including your region, ad availability, and consent settings. Please ensure you have granted consent for personalized ads if prompted.
*   **Q: How do I delete my account?**
    *   A: You can delete your account and all associated data directly within the app. Navigate to **Settings > Danger Zone > Delete Account**. Please be aware this action is irreversible.
